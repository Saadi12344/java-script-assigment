About Us

Learn more about this project and the idea behind it.

Our Story

MySite started as a course assignment focused on building and deploying a simple multi-page website. The idea was to keep things clean and practical: a Home page that introduces the site, an About page that explains its purpose, and a Contact page where visitors can reach out.

Our Mission

To demonstrate solid front-end fundamentals — structured HTML, organized CSS, and a consistent navigation experience — while keeping the design approachable and easy to extend for future projects.
